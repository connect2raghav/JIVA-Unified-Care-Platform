# JIVA Poster Copy

## Headline

**One calm command center for every clinic handoff.**

## Problem

Clinic teams lose time moving between disconnected registers, phone calls, and spreadsheets while appointments, transport requests, blood stock, and nearby facilities change throughout the day.

## Solution

JIVA brings the operational picture into one shared workspace. Teams can register patients, move appointments through a clear status board, coordinate emergency transport, monitor blood inventory, and look up nearby facilities without leaving the clinic workflow.

## Key features

- Patient and appointment coordination
- Emergency request and ambulance status tracking
- Blood stock visibility across groups and components
- Clinic-scoped facilities directory with search and contact actions
- Role-aware access with Supabase row-level security
- Cloud Run-ready deployment architecture

## Boundary

JIVA supports administrative coordination. It does not diagnose, triage, or recommend treatment.
