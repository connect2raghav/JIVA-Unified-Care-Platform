## Post

Healthcare coordination should not depend on scattered registers and last-minute calls.

Meet **JIVA**, a unified care platform for clinic operations. JIVA connects patient registration, appointment flow, emergency transport coordination, blood inventory visibility, and a searchable nearby-facilities directory in one administrative workspace.

The goal is simple: give clinic teams a clear operational view so handoffs are easier to coordinate and less information gets lost between desks.

JIVA is designed for administrative coordination only. It does not diagnose, triage, or provide treatment recommendations.
